🏠 Airbnb Global Performance Dashboard
📊 Project Overview

An end-to-end Power BI data analytics project transforming raw Airbnb data into an interactive 3-page dashboard to analyze global growth, market share, customer satisfaction, and review behavior.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Power BI Desktop – Dashboard & Visualization
Power Query – Data Cleaning & ETL
DAX – KPIs, cumulative analysis & calculations
Data Modeling – Relationships & semantic modeling
📌 Dashboard Pages
1. Overview – Growth & Trends
Total listings, cities & reviews KPIs
Airbnb growth analysis (2008–2020)
Room-type segmentation
2. Ratings – Market Share & Feedback
City-wise market share analysis
Overall & detailed rating views
Cleanliness, Accuracy & Communication analysis
Conditional formatting for city ratings
3. Reviews – Customer Behavior
Reviews per reviewer analysis
Monthly review seasonality across top cities
Host verification & trust analysis
💡 Key Insights
Paris leads in listing volume.
86.5% of customers leave only one review, indicating a repeat-engagement opportunity.
60%+ of hosts are fully verified, supporting customer trust.
Paris, New York & Sydney account for a significant share of global listings.
